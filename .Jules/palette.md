## 2025-07-07 - LogOut Button Accessibility & Focus
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons lacking `aria-label` and `title` fail to communicate their purpose to screen readers and mouse users. Furthermore, using `focus:outline-none` without providing an alternative like `focus-visible` completely breaks keyboard navigation visibility. Adding these simple attributes and classes dramatically improves the accessibility and usability of interactive elements.
**Action:** When creating or modifying icon-only buttons, always ensure they have localized `aria-label` and `title` attributes (e.g., "Se déconnecter" for French), set `aria-hidden="true"` on the SVG itself, and replace plain `focus:outline-none` with explicit `focus-visible` styling that matches the design token (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-1 focus-visible:ring-white/30`).
