## 2024-11-20 - [AppShell Sidebar Logout Button Accessibility]
**Learning:** Icon-only action buttons in dark sidebar footers (like the AppShell) lack sufficient keyboard navigation cues and screen reader context. The `focus-visible` utility is required for keyboard navigation, and its ring offset color must explicitly match the surrounding background (e.g., `#030303`) to prevent clipping. Additionally, French localization (e.g., 'Se déconnecter') is standard for accessibility labels in this app.
**Action:** When adding or auditing icon-only buttons, always apply `aria-label`, `title`, and `aria-hidden="true"` to the child SVG. For keyboard accessibility in dark themes, explicitly map `focus-visible` offset colors to the exact background hex to ensure visibility without artifacts.
