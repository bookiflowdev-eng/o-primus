## 2024-05-26 - Missing Aria Labels on Icon-only Buttons
**Learning:** Many icon-only buttons in the application (like FolderArchive, LayoutTemplate, Settings2 in OmniBar) lack `aria-label` or `title` attributes, making them inaccessible to screen readers and confusing for users without context.
**Action:** Always add `title` and `aria-label` to icon-only buttons to improve accessibility and usability.
