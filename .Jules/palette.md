## 2024-05-24 - Accessibility of Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like the `AppShell` LogOut button) are completely inaccessible to screen readers, tooltip-reliant pointer users, and keyboard navigators without explicit attributes. They rely solely on visual cues that fail for assistive technologies and lack sufficient keyboard focus indicators, making navigation ambiguous.
**Action:** Always add `aria-label` and `title` attributes (using localized strings, e.g., 'Se déconnecter') to icon-only buttons. Pair `focus:outline-none` with explicit `focus-visible` fallback classes (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-white/30 focus-visible:ring-offset-1 focus-visible:ring-offset-black`) to ensure clear keyboard accessibility against dark backgrounds.
