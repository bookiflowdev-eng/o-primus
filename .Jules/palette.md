## 2024-05-24 - Focus Rings on Dark Themes and Icon-only Buttons
**Learning:** Adding accessible focus states (`focus-visible`) on deep dark mode backgrounds (e.g., `#030303`, `#050505`) requires exact matching `ring-offset` colors so the focus ring remains visually distinct without looking like a CSS glitch. Additionally, standalone icons acting as functional buttons must have an `aria-label` and `title` to be usable by screen readers and standard cursor users, and their inner SVGs should be given `aria-hidden="true"`.
**Action:** When overriding `focus:outline-none`, always supplement with `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-[current_bg_hex]`. When encountering icon-only buttons, instantly check for and apply `aria-label`/`title` and explicitly hide the nested SVG from the accessibility tree.
